Open-Meteo does not use ground stations. Forecasts are interpolated from gridded weather models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON) at ~3–25 km resolution. The "Model DEM elevation" is what the model thinks the ground is at your coords; passing your real elevation as an override applies a temperature lapse-rate correction.

Use this if your resort already has a weather feed. Point us at any JSON endpoint, then map its fields to ours. Works with any shape — internal ops API, Snocountry feed, on-mountain sensors, custom JSON. Polled every 15 minutes.
